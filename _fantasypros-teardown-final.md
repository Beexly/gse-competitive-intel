## ACCURACY-AUDIT

**Summary:** Re-audited FantasyPros' full expert-accuracy stack (four products: Draft/preseason, In-Season weekly, Rest-of-Season, and NFL Mock Draft) against the live public methodology pages and the 2025 leaderboard preview. The crown-jewel finding is structural and net-new: the system NEVER scores an expert's own numeric projection. It converts every expert's RANK into a rank-slot historical-average point value, so the metric is fundamentally ordinal. In the Draft product this is doubly synthetic — BOTH the predicted side (expert's rank-slot 3-yr average) AND the "actual" side (the player's finish-rank-slot 3-yr average) are smoothed curve values, so Draft "Accuracy Gap" collapses to a rank-distance metric |f(pred_rank) − f(finish_rank)| in smoothed-point space; it literally cannot reward predicting a player's real point magnitude, only finishing order. Because the points-vs-rank curve is steep at the top and flat through the middle/tail, misranks among the interchangeable middle are nearly free while the score is dominated by nailing the obvious studs — quantifying the primary teardown's "rank toward the low-variance middle." Three additional mechanisms bias the leaderboard toward consensus-hugging: (a) the injured-player penalty only fires if your gap is worse than the field average, granting full immunity for mistakes the herd also makes; (b) the In-Season z-score + drop-worst-week aggregation is field-relative and rewards low weekly variance over peak accuracy; (c) the 2021 Draft weighting halves the value of deep/sleeper accuracy. Reconciling the prior teardown: FantasyPros HAS closed the raw omission loophole with explicit last-ranked+1 / worse-of(ECR+1, last+1) assignment rules and an injured-player penalty, but the omission cost stays mild in the flat tail, and the deeper ordinal-only, herd-immunity, and consistency-selecting seams remain fully open. Separately confirmed: the flagship ECR shows no public evidence of being accuracy-weighted — the product they sell discards the accuracy signal they compute — and the whole badge is Half-PPR-locked and excludes K/DST/IDP from Overall.

## ECR-CONSENSUS

**Summary:** FantasyPros' Expert Consensus Ranking (ECR) is NOT a simple average, a median, or an accuracy-weighted blend. Their own Tools FAQ (re-scraped 2026-07-15, still current) states ECR is a Borda-count-style "Rank Points" tally: each expert's rank slot for a player converts to Rank Points (better slot = more points), points are summed across all experts, and players are ordered by total. They explicitly reject averaging ("We don't use an 'average rank' because... [it] requires that you assign an arbitrary rank for unranked players, which skews the results"). The default ECR weights every tracked expert EQUALLY; expert accuracy does NOT feed back into it — accuracy weighting is deliberately kept OPT-IN via three surfaces (Cheat Sheet Wizard accuracy filters, hand-picking experts, and a separate "Most Accurate Fantasy Football Rankings" product). This VERIFIES and sharpens the team's prior finding for current 2026 state. Around ECR sit four distinct, separable layers: (1) Tiers = Gaussian Mixture Model clustering (the Boris Chen method) applied on top of ECR; (2) ADP = a composite average of REAL draft position across league hosts (ESPN/Yahoo/Sleeper/Underdog/CBS), surfaced as an "ECR vs ADP" value-gap column — opinion vs market behavior; (3) VBD/VOR = a separate projection-driven track (Zeile Projections + VORP for positional scarcity), NOT derived from consensus; (4) Strength-of-Schedule and matchup Points-Allowed = contextual layers, not part of ECR construction. Rankings and projections are two independent pipelines. The decisive structural weakness is stated in their own marketing: consensus is sold as RISK MINIMIZATION ("naturally weed out outlier opinions," "a safer alternative," "minimize the risk of following bold but inaccurate predictions") — i.e., regression to the mainstream and no conviction by design. Two more concrete seams: (a) admitted freshness lag — ECR updates daily but "most experts do not update their rankings daily," so default ECR is only as current as its slowest contributors; and (b) the ONLY accuracy claim for ECR-as-a-product is a 16-year-old, self-reported "finished in the Top 5 among all experts in 2010" — they prove EXPERTS' accuracy, never the current accuracy of the consensus itself.
