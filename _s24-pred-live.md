## Viborg Team Overview

The visitors last took to the pitch on in a Club Friendly, facing Viborg away from home. Viborg came out on top with a 2-0 victory over Notts County.

Here’s how the stats broke down (Viborg vs Notts County):

- Possession: 52% vs 48%;
- Corners: 4 vs 4;
- Yellow cards: 2 vs 3;
- Shots on target: 3 vs 2.

Viborg are approaching this preseason in a completely different manner. With no European commitments, the coaching staff has the luxury of taking their time with preparations and experimenting extensively with the lineup. In almost every friendly, they field two separate elevens, one for the first half, and a fresh set after the break. At this stage, fast counter-attacks remain Viborg’s main weapon as they gear up for the new campaign.
