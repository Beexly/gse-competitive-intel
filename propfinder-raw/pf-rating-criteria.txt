OBSERVATIONS
  1. 35 of 100 points (E, M, x) are BINARY step functions on "is the average above the line".
     A player averaging 0.01 over the line scores identically to one averaging 10 over.
  2. 6 of 9 components are restatements of the same recent-form series
     (season/L5/L10 x average/hit-rate). Heavy collinearity; systematically chases form.
  3. Missing data DEFAULTS TO THE MIDPOINT (S=5 of 10, b=5 of 10) rather than abstaining --
     absent information silently earns half credit.
  4. "L10 Defensive Matchup" is a raw ordinal rank percentile (15 * rank/D), with no
     opponent-strength magnitude, only order.
