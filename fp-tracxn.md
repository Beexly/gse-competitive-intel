## FantasyPros - About the company

FantasyPros is an unfunded company based in Las Vegas (United States), founded in 2010 by [David Kim](https://platform.tracxn.com/a/d/people/57b57a35e4b0b47ace8e71a4/davidkim) and [Tom Nguyen](https://platform.tracxn.com/a/d/people/6a44ddc16fbe944e1f97346d/tomnguyen). It operates as a Fantasy sports insights, rankings, projections, and tools for informed decisions. FantasyPros has not raised any funding yet. The company has 94 active competitors, including 9 funded and 8 that have exited. Its top competitors include companies like [RotoBaller](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/rotoballer/__Ryk31fVkVTrDCXSNYTc1O5gAB6hkP5BraJE1y34Gxzw), [RotoWire](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/rotowire/__ZP99n1eqz-fWD-c9TsCwuiweiHKdxdF_UIQiWTjEnL4) and [Fanalyze](https://tracxn.com/d/companies/fanalyze/__2xKqYrkKPmQZ4azW0-v3XEBYKs6eFRu3wL3LBz2eUJw).

### Company Details

Fantasy sports insights, rankings, projections, and tools for informed decisions. This is a platform providing fantasy sports enthusiasts with data-driven insights and tools to enhance their decision-making. The service offers rankings, projections, cheat sheets, and expert analysis for NFL, MLB, NBA, and NHL fantasy leagues. Users can access free content and tools, with options for premium subscriptions to unlock enhanced features. The platform collects user data, including personal and league information, to personalize the experience and improve relevance. It utilizes cookies for various purposes, including storing preferences and measuring advertising effectiveness. The platform prioritizes data security and user privacy, implementing security measures and adhering to privacy regulations. It offers a range of resources, including mobile apps, news desk, and shop, catering to the diverse needs of fantasy sports players. The business model is subscription based.

What does FantasyPros do?

Fantasy sports insights, rankings, projections, and tools for informed decisions. This is a platform providing fantasy sports enthusiasts with data-driven insights and tools to enhance their decision-making. The service offers rankings, projections, cheat sheets, and expert analysis for NFL, MLB, NBA, and NHL fantasy leagues. Users can access free content and tools, with options for premium subscriptions to unlock enhanced features. The platform collects user data, including personal and league information, to personalize the experience and improve relevance. It utilizes cookies for various purposes, including storing preferences and measuring advertising effectiveness. The platform prioritizes data security and user privacy, implementing security measures and adhering to privacy regulations. It offers a range of resources, including mobile apps, news desk, and shop, catering to the diverse needs of fantasy sports players. The business model is subscription based.
