# About Us

Since 2010, FantasyPros® has aggregated and rated fantasy sports expert advice from around the web to make it easy for you to make the best decisions while drafting, managing your team, or playing daily fantasy sports. Our innovative tools and content provide the easiest way to get the latest news and most accurate advice to help you win at fantasy sports.

**For Fantasy Players:**

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/ECR-Icon.png)**Expert Consensus Rankings**](http://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/consensus.php) ™

Don’t trust any one expert? We consolidate expert rankings into one ECR™ (Expert Consensus Ranking™). Consensus advice helps minimize the risk of following bold but inaccurate predictions. Our fantasy football ECR is updated daily and we support Standard, Half PPR, PPR, and Custom scoring.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Draft-Wizard-Icon.png)**Draft Wizard**](http://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/?partner=fpros) ®

Our award-winning suite of draft tools that syncs with your league to help you prepare for and dominate your draft. Featuring fast and realistic mock draft simulations, cheat sheet creation and management, in-depth draft analysis, and the Draft Assistant, which gives you live, expert-driven advice during your draft.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/MPB-Icon.png)**My Playbook**](http://www.fantasypros.com/myplaybook/)

Save time while winning championships with the most powerful in-season fantasy management suite available, including team dashboards, lineup tools, waiver advice, trade analysis, and multi-league assistance.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Breaking-News-Icon.png)**Breaking News**](http://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/player-news.php)

We monitor hundreds of sources across the internet for fantasy news on the specific topics you’re looking for. Our team correspondents provide up-to-the-minute analysis of how the news impacts your fantasy squads.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Accuracy-Ratings-Icon.png)**Accuracy Ratings**](http://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/accuracy/)

We track and measure the accuracy of the top fantasy football experts by using an innovative system that analyzes millions of their player predictions. Our objective system analyzes both draft and in-season rankings.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/WSI-Icon.png)**Who Should I Draft, Start, or Trade?**](http://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/players/)

Whether you’re having a tough time deciding between players to start or just want advice on a particular player, our “Who Should I” tools instantly give you all the information you need. You’ll get the experts’ recommendations along with detailed analysis to help you make the right decision.

[![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Mobile-Apps-Icon.png)**Mobile Apps**](https://www.fantasypros.com/fantasy-mobile-apps/)

All of our tools are available as apps for your iPhone or Android device. Practice drafting, creating your own rankings, get advice, and stay up to date on the latest fantasy relevant news all on your mobile device.

[Feedback](mailto:info@fantasypros.com) is always welcome. Thanks for your support!

**For Fantasy Experts:**

![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Expert-Profiles-Icon.png)**Expert Profile Page**

Be a part of our comprehensive directory of the top fantasy sports professionals. Your profile page will include a short bio and links to your website, blog, Twitter, and Facebook accounts. We’ll also showcase your advice, rankings, and accuracy results.

**![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Promotion-Icon.png)Promotion in our Tools**

We provide exposure by promoting your advice in our popular tools. Our audience will see your name and recommendations next to the most well known sites and experts in the industry. Best of all, this promotion requires no additional work on your part.

**![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Accuracy-Competition-Icon.png)Accuracy Competitions**

For fantasy prognosticators that produce and publish player rankings that meet our minimum requirements, we offer inclusion in our seasonal accuracy contests. This provides a level playing field for you to compete with some of the most well known experts in our industry.

**![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Player-Ranker-Icon.png)Online Player Ranker**

Our ranking tool makes it easy for you to produce, revise, and publish your cheat sheets. Access the tool anytime from anywhere, and publish to your site or blog with a push of a button.

**![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Smart-Content-Icon.png)Smart Content for Your Site**

We provide some of our industry-leading content for use on your website. One example is our Rankings Widget. It’s a fully automated widget that provides the latest Expert Consensus Rankings to your audience. Just cut and paste one line of code and you’re set.

**![](https://cdn.fantasypros.com/wp-content/uploads/Links-and-Traffic-Icon.png)Links and Traffic**

The service we provide to fantasy players will result in more exposure for you. Many of our services provide valuable links to your site and social accounts, which can be helpful for search engine visibility and audience growth.

If you’re a fantasy professional or site and want to learn more about participating with us, please [contact us](mailto:partners@fantasypros.com) via email.
