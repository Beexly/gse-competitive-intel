★ Expert Consensus Rankings (ECR)

Make the best decisions with expert consensus rankings from the top fantasy experts injected into your favorite fantasy league hosts and websites.

★ Find the Best Sleepers

Our extension matches player names on all of your favorite fantasy sites so you can quickly access the latest news, notes, and advice for any player, from any site.

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/Lidipwfwj5OG9J3bXxmPYivfNXIzR-78GAdNfyoxzX0-9SOguau1gafUIIURTLlM9GNlApA=w640-h400-e365)

★ Top Available Players

Quickly see the top available players in your league, based on FantasyPros Expert Consensus Rankings.

★ Start the Right Players

Set your optimal lineup with a single click, based on the consensus opinions of the top fantasy experts. (FantasyPros Premium MVP/HOF users only)

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/j5MhflI3-K2zRYXtIopsmK-7xY6Zt4H-LWDjWECK1g7QN-1guVU8S_DCdReG5LlnUgPSrGKrlg=w640-h400-e365)

★ Expert Waiver Analysis

Get immediate expert analysis on any waiver transaction, without leaving your league host. (FantasyPros Premium MVP/HOF/GOAT users only)

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/S8knGGxmsHJX0JGi_5qgyeaInbnxaOyj2yoFHlxnVCMiZTXx96Rfd7zvl05LxoilPSXbJA_7=w640-h400-e365)

★ Consensus Projections

Leverage our set of projections that are powered by multiple sources.

★ Matchup Analysis

See the star ratings based on each opponent's Fantasy Points Allowed to a position, adjusted for strength of schedule.

★ Player Availability

Instantly view player availability in all your fantasy leagues, from any site. (FantasyPros Premium MVP/HOF/GOAT users only)

![](https://lh3.googleusercontent.com/3cN4BgB0osxtKjvcoifDvxhfGOzPCRZAJg3FkCnxMUWeB2GS_oWGQZrle4IPagnUj2PSFb7yyQ=w640-h400-e365)

★ League Host Support

In-season features currently support Yahoo, ESPN, NFL.com, MyFantasyLeague.com, and CBS leagues.
