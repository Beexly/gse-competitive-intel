## Overview

Securely sync supported third-party account data to your BP dashboard for unified performance tracking.

BP Account Sync Utility securely connects supported third-party accounts to your BP profile to simplify performance monitoring and data organization. The extension enables the import of account information that users already have access to on external platforms, allowing BP to provide a consolidated view for personal tracking and insights. BP Account Sync Utility does not enable wagering, contests, or any real-money gaming activity. It serves solely as a data-connection tool to help users view their own authorized account information within BP.

★ Key Features
\- Securely link supported external accounts to your BP dashboard
\- View a unified summary of your performance and activity
\- Maintain consistent and up-to-date information across platforms
\- No betting, gaming, or contest functionality of any kind

★ Important
BP Account Sync Utility is not a gambling product and does not facilitate wagering. It only connects user-authorized data from permitted third-party services for the purpose of account organization and personal performance tracking.
