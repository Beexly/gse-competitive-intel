# What is the Draft Analyzer?

![AJ Adams](https://support.fantasypros.com/system/photos/7342684956955/Headshot.jpg)



AJ Adams






-
Updated
2 years ago

The Draft Analyzer provides an instant analysis of your mock and real drafts. It gives you a quick idea of how your team stacks up and breaks down your strengths and weaknesses. The Summary page displays your Projected Standings (Overall, Starters, and Bench), Your Position Ranks, and Draft Insights.

![](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/22744982580507)

The Expert Opinions tab shows which Experts like your draft the most and which like it the least.

![](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/22744982605339)

The Pick Analysis tab allows you to compare and analyze your pick against other picks that could have improved your team.

![](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/22744956977563)

Have some experts that you prefer? You can analyze your draft with custom experts by clicking the Pick Experts button and selecting the experts of your choice.

![](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/22744956996635)
