## Marketing

1

**Email:** Dedicated emails and content blocks. Control your creative, titles, copy, and CTAs to one of our many email lists.

2

**Digital Advertising:** Full page ad takeovers and interstitials for a dedicated period for users accessing our site and apps.

3

**Product Sponsorship:** Have your company sponsor one of our products or tools, featured throughout the site and mobile apps.

## Media

1

**Livestream and podcast sponsorship :** Have your company spotlighted on one of our many livestreams and podcasts, including on-video branding, live host reads, pinned comments in chat sections, and copy & links in description sections.

2

**Articles :** Weekly articles series highlighting your product, game strategy, writer's picks, and more hosted on of our sports pages.

3

**Social Media:** Dedicated social media posts, including full copy, related creative, and tracking links to our 600,000+ followers across social media channels.

## Tools & Integrations

1

**Co-branded Mock Draft Simulator :** Capitalize on the most intuitive mock draft tool in the industry, where your users can complete fully customizable and realistic mock drafts in minutes.

2

**Draft Grades and Projected Standings:** Serve your users a FantasyPros-powered grade and projected standing, based on our consensus and/or your individually-selected experts for both mock and real drafts.

3

**Draft Insights:** Post-draft, users will receive Draft Insights that detail ways they could have improved their draft, steals & reaches, and more.
