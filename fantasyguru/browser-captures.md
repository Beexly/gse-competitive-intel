## Pricing takeaways (for GSE)
- Football All-In ($499.99) is ~2.5× Baseball All-In ($199.99) — fantasy football is the profit center; baseball is priced to convert/retain.
- À-la-carte modular pricing (Seasonal / DFS / Betting split) lets them upsell single-vertical users; add-ons (Training Camp, Franchise Mode, Elite Data) are the margin layer.
- "Elite Data" is monetized separately ($19.99/mo) — the props/ownership/edge data is treated as a premium standalone.
- Heavy reliance on personality brand (Jeff Mans/Ray Flowers) + SiriusXM distribution + Discord community as the moat, not just data.
