# scores24.live — Verified GSE Offensive Playbook (2026-07-16)

## Executive summary
scores24.live is a Russian-origin, Estonian-shell (Kiito OÜ, reg. 14346593, sole owner Evgenii Kirillov) betting-affiliate SEO machine wearing an international skin. Its power is scale, not substance: ~600,000 programmatic pages across 13 languages, ~4.36M visits/mo (+29% MoM), a mature real-time stack (Centrifugo/protobuf WebSockets, dedicated parser tier, self-built GraphQL), monetized through cloaked Keitaro CPA funnels (tvsv.ru/tvsv.pro) steering US traffic to unlicensed offshore/crypto books (BetUS, Bovada, MyBookie, Stake) at 18+ gating. But it is a trust laggard on every axis GSE cares about. Its headline "confidence" is a recent-form STREAK hit-rate structurally trapped at 80-100% and provably DECOUPLED from market-implied probability (verified: a 100% badge on a bet the market prices at 52.6%; a 92% badge on a -139/58% pick). It publishes ZERO own accuracy — no win rate, ROI, CLV, calibration, or settled-tip ledger anywhere on the site; its only feedback loop is a near-empty "will this come true? Yes/No" poll. Its data is self-scraped SofaScore-lineage stats with no attribution; its infra rides Yandex Metrica/AdFox and Russian CDN; the "prediction" and the affiliate click are literally the same object (undisclosed incentive conflict). Critically, the US is only ~10% of its (LatAm/Russia-core) traffic — Similarweb literally files it under "Gambling" — leaving the premium US trust lane essentially uncontested. GSE cannot and should not out-page a 600k-URL machine that is simultaneously the exact templated-content class AI Overviews are cratering. GSE wins by inverting every axis: a calibrated GSE Rating whose number IS a probability bound to a public settled win-rate ledger; per-datapoint source citation; US entity/hosting/analytics with no Russian dependencies and no hidden affiliate; and distribution via AI-citation, brand/direct, and subscription rather than dying programmatic SEO. Their inflated certainty is the single sharpest, screenshot-able trust gift in the entire category.
