# Do you store my password when syncing my league?

![Dan Martin](https://support.fantasypros.com/system/photos/52811106342939/daniel-martin-jr-headshot.png)



Dan Martin






-
Updated
2 years ago

For most league hosts, we do not store your password. For **CBS**, **NFL.com**, **MyFantasyLeague**, **Reality Sports Online**, and **RTSports** leagues, we do store your username and password as encrypted values in our secure database. This allows our tools to connect to your league during the draft and throughout the season.
