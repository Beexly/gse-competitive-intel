## Astana Team Overview

The Kazakh side head into the second leg as clear favourites. Grigori Babayan’s men are riding a five-match home winning streak, having kept three clean sheets during that run. After their victory in Albania, Astana edged out Aktobe 3-2 in the Kazakhstan Premier League. Still, they currently sit only third in the table, trailing second-placed Kairat by ten points.

The first leg in Elbasan ended in a win for Astana, but it would be a stretch to say they dominated Dinamo. In fact, as is often the case for visitors, they spent much of the match on the back foot, conceding possession (45% to 55%). They also created fewer dangerous attacks (48 to 75), but Ramazan Karimov seized his chance midway through the second half to score his first goal since May.

## Dinamo City Team Overview

The Albanian side find themselves in a tough spot after suffering a home defeat. Having lost on their own turf, Dinamo City now need to chase victory in Astana, where the artificial pitch has proven troublesome for many visiting teams. Ilir Daja’s men looked solid during pre-season friendlies, going unbeaten throughout, but European competition presents a different level of intensity and pace.

That said, Dinamo showed some positive signs - they tried to take the initiative, fully aware that much would hinge on their home result. Still, they couldn’t find the back of the net. It’s worth noting that the Tirana club have historically struggled in the Europa Conference League, managing just two wins from seven matches in the competition. Dinamo City have also lost their last two away fixtures in this tournament - falling to Hajduk (1-2) and Jagiellonia (0-3).
