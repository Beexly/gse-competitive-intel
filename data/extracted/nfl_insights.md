## Key insights

1. **The only public NFL model with a season-by-season ledger is nfelo, and its numbers must be versioned, not merged.** Live today the page reports all-time SU 66.72%, ATS vs open 55.90%, ATS vs close 55.06%, CLV 5.85%, 134.0 units, 2009–2025 (17 seasons) (https://www.nfeloapp.com/games/nfl-model-performance/, fetched 2026-09-08 19:33 UTC). The 2026-09-04 wave read the same page as 66.72% / 55.06% / 5.86% / +133.0 units / 2009–2024 (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `public_benchmarks[0]`), and the Wave-1 owner brief carried 66.61% SU / 53.70% ATS-vs-close / +5.61% CLV (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `delta_report.wave_1_summary[3]`; `_MASTER-gse-strategic-dossier.md:64` still prints "~66.6% SU, ~53.7% ATS-vs-close, +5.61% CLV"). Three readings, three dates, one moving self-reported ledger: the 2025 season was added between reads (SU 65.1%, ATS open 57.1% +9.8u, ATS close 55.6% +6.0u, CLV 3.53%) (nfeloapp.com, fetched 2026-09-08 19:33 UTC). The wave itself records "game-level audit NOT performed" (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `public_benchmarks[0].sample_size`), and the Sports registry lists nfelo's outputs as verdict `forbidden` with "No license granted on their outputs" (Sports/packages/data-ingestion/src/source-registry.ts:378-392). Cite nfelo as a self-reported benchmark with a fetch date, never as a ceiling and never as an input.

2. **Straight-up accuracy and betting accuracy decouple, and nfelo's own 2024 row proves it.** nfelo's 2024 season reads SU 69.1% but ATS vs close 45.3% for −14.2 units (https://www.nfeloapp.com/games/nfl-model-performance/, fetched 2026-09-08 19:33 UTC). A model that named the winner more often than its lifetime average lost money against the spread that year. The market's favourite already wins 66.8% straight-up over 1989–2012 with roughly a 10.3-point average spread miss (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `public_benchmarks[2]`, citing inpredictable.com), and sportsbook spreads explain r²=0.86 of median-margin variability across 5,412 regular-season games 2002–2022 (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `public_benchmarks[3]`, citing PMC10306238). The wave's own defensible-claim guidance: "GSE should not advertise '70% ATS long-term' or imply SU equals betting profit" (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `defensible_claim_threshold`).

3. **The founder's ">75%" figure is a TARGET, not a benchmark, and no cited source reaches it.** The highest SU figure in the corpus is nfelo's 2024 season at 69.1% (fetched 2026-09-08 19:33 UTC); the market favourite baseline is 66.8% (waves `public_benchmarks[2]`); the intel doctrine caps the blind all-games ATS rate at "~52–56%" and allows "~57–60% ... ONLY as a SELECTIVE, props-heavy number on ~8–15% coverage" after "+CLV over 200+ fired bets" (`_HANDOFF-to-coding-agent.md` §3). Anything above 70% SU across a full NFL slate would exceed every public model in the corpus; no source documents a way to get there, so the file records ">75%" as an aspiration with no supporting number.

4. **GSE's own frozen model, replayed on 27 NFL seasons, is at break-even on hit rate and negative on money.** Run 2026-09-04 on nflverse `games.csv` (CC-BY-4.0): 6,967 games, 15,939 settled picks, 15,647 decisive, 52.700% win rate, 95% CI [51.92%, 53.48%] against a 52.381% break-even at −110, z 0.799, not significant in any window (Sports/docs/data/NFL_REPLAY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-04.md:19-38). By market: SPREAD n 6,778 at 48.86% (ROI −6.53%), TOTAL n 6,868 at 49.49% (ROI −5.44%), MONEYLINE n 2,001 at 76.71% (ROI −1.96%) because moneylines publish only on heavy favourites "empirically around −350"; overall ROI −5.48% (Sports/docs/data/NFL_REPLAY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-04.md:58-70). By era 1999–2005 48.90%, 2006–2012 49.86%, 2013–2019 49.22%, 2020–2025 48.70%: "the spread/total model has never beaten the close" (Sports/docs/data/NFL_REPLAY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-04.md:155-162). The 2025-alone 53.968% on 630 picks has CI [50.08%, 57.86%] and "should not be quoted on its own" (Sports/docs/data/NFL_REPLAY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-04.md:33-44).

5. **On NFL the confidence score is mildly anti-informative, and confidence is the paywall.** 70–79 confidence n 3,770 at 48.33% ROI −7.52% versus 65–69 n 9,693 at 49.47% ROI −5.46%; "a pick labelled 'high conviction' was, on 27 seasons of history, the worse bet" and this is "a direct problem for `PREMIUM_CONFIDENCE_THRESHOLD = 70`" (Sports/docs/data/NFL_REPLAY_CALIBRATION_2026-09-04.md:81-94). The replay AUC on 13,646 spread/total picks is 0.4965 with permutation p 0.4113, and the controls (|line| 0.4982, rest 0.5019, week 0.5073) all sit at 0.50 (Sports/docs/data/CONVERGENT_CALIBRATION_EVIDENCE_2026-09-04.md:15). Live, the market's de-vigged probability beats every GSE score on Brier (0.2337 vs 0.2474), ECE (0.0280 vs 0.0838) and Murphy resolution (0.0185 vs 0.0112), with the row sets not identical (market coverage 0.774 vs confidence 1.000) (data/raw/HANDOFF_20260908.md §1c).

6. **Live NFL calibration is n 28 with ECE 0.267, and that is noise, not a direction.** "NFL n 28 ECE 0.267 ... roughly three picks in a bin, so both the magnitude and the sign are dominated by sampling noise"; an earlier "under-confident" inference was withdrawn (Sports/AGENTS.md:70-78). Under a perfect-calibration null the plug-in ECE floor at n 28 is 0.151 (K_eff 4), 0.169 (K_eff 5), 0.239 (K_eff 10), "a derivation under a stated model, not a measurement" (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:136-155). The literature's answer to thin strata is Mondrian / group-conditional conformal coverage per sport × market (wave2/forecast-scoring-literature.md:85).

7. **What actually drives GSE's NFL win probability today is the market, plus one public efficiency rating.** The composite weights are consensus 30, market depth 20, edge 25, line movement ±15, volatility −15, head-to-head 5, venue form 5, uncertainty −8, cross-market +4/−3, schedule stress 5 (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/constants.ts:47-88). The NFL-specific independent estimate is opponent-adjusted EPA/play from nflverse `TeamGameEfficiency` through a logistic: `m = overall_home − overall_away + HFA`, `p_home = sigmoid(m / scale)` with `NFL_EPA_HFA = 0.025` EPA/play, `NFL_EPA_MARGIN_SCALE = 0.12`, `NFL_EPA_MIN_GAMES = 4` (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/nfl-epa-fair-value.ts:1-26); the rating is an iterative offense↔defense opponent adjustment, "a DVOA/SRS-family rating, our recipe" (Sports/packages/prediction-engine/src/opponent-adjusted.ts:1-13), aggregated at ingestion from ten play-by-play columns (Sports/apps/web/lib/ingestion/team-efficiency.ts:1-18). ESPN FPI enters only when `ESPN_POWERINDEX_LICENSED="true"`, default closed (Sports/packages/ingestion-pipeline/src/build-independent-fair-values.ts:7-9; independent-source-rights.ts:22-26). The corpus carries no ranked feature-importance study for NFL win probability beyond these; the two public peers document their inputs only: Inpredictable "weighted linear regression on season point spreads, with recency weights; NFL credibility coefficient is 15%" (waves/wave2-extract-data-2026-09-04.json `competitor_teardowns[2].methodology_claims`), FiveThirtyEight "pregame home/away Elo, win probabilities, QB-adjusted ratings" (waves `competitor_teardowns[3].methodology_claims`).

Player level: `HOME_BONUS = 0.015` (+1.5% on projected fantasy points), `SHORT_WEEK_PENALTY = 0.03` (−3% under 6 days' rest), `TOTAL_MAX = 0.15` (±15% for Vegas implied total), `LEAGUE_AVG_TOTAL = 22.5`, `OPPONENT_MAX = 0.12` (Sports/apps/web/lib/projections/weekly-model.ts:94-100). No external, cited home-field point value exists anywhere in the read corpus: NOT CONFIRMED.

9. **Injury effect sizes: measured once, on synthetic data, and the estimate includes zero.** R-10, a double machine-learning causal prototype on QB out/limited, shadow-only, reports ATT −0.0111 with a CI that includes 0 and a placebo containing 0, on a "synthetic panel (no nflverse dump in workspace)" (Sports/docs/ops/AGENT_LEDGER.md:185). The edge-lab rates a causal lineup/injury counterfactual method [marginal], "+0 to +200 bps on a thin (~5–10% of weekly markets) post-news slice, decaying in minutes-to-hours" (`_gse-edge-lab-final.md:144`) — a projection, not a measurement. What exists in code: official Out/Doubtful/Questionable designations from the nflverse `injuries` release, with `injuries_2026.csv` "often 404" as of 2026-08 so 2025 remains the honest last complete season (Sports/apps/web/lib/nflverse/injury-report.ts:9-21); depth charts naming the beneficiary when a starter is Out (Sports/apps/web/lib/nflverse/depth-charts.ts:2-6); and the weekly model's rule that availability "only widens the band, never shifts the mean; an official 'Out' zeroes the projection" (Sports/apps/web/lib/projections/weekly-model.ts:14-16). An injury effect size in points or probability is NOT CONFIRMED anywhere in the corpus.

11. **The literature gives usable NFL distribution facts, and one of them is measured, not folklore.** Shapiro-Wilk cannot reject normality for 76% of QBs 2016–2018 and "nearly half the time" for any position with expected score ≥ 10 (wave2/simulation-literature.md:61-68, citing arXiv 2112.07002 §6.2.3). The nearest-neighbour recipe uses the 50 same-position players with closest projection for variance and 50 same-relationship pairs for correlation, then a PSD correction (wave2/simulation-literature.md:136-156). Measured stack correlations over 2022–2025 on "more than 1,300 team-stack seasons": QB–WR +0.31, QB–TE +0.27, QB–RB +0.07, same-team WR–WR −0.02; a QB–WR stack adds "roughly 1.8 points" of weekly ceiling and removes "about 1.6 points" of floor, is "about 14% more boom-or-bust", and "over a full season, stacking does not change how many points you score" (https://www.rotowire.com/football/article/does-stacking-work-in-fantasy-football-what-four-years-of-data-say-about-drafting-correlated-players-2026-131409, Tyler Huntington, updated 2026-09-03, fetched 2026-09-08 19:35 UTC). The folklore "+0.6 or higher" comes "from single-game DFS slates where one shootout drives everything" (wave2/simulation-literature.md:189-201).

**Do well**
