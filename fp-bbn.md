## Company Traction

- As of July 2023, FantasyPros gets 4.9 million monthly visitors with an average visit duration of 4 minutes and 40 seconds.

- In 2020, FantasyPros reported 38 million annual visitors, 2.22 billion impressions, 1.2 million email subscribers, and an average time on site of 5 minutes and 8 seconds.
