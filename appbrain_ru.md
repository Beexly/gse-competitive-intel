**Description**

Scores24 is an informational application for sports fans. For you - detailed descriptions and analytics of sporting events, convenient schedules, and results online. Learn statistics, make predictions, and set up live notifications to keep up with the progress of matches.

In addition to the functionality you'll appreciate thoughtful navigation. We put a ton of important information into a convenient menu and polished it with a stylish design. It is important for us that the user experience exceed expectations.

• Find your games of interest (soccer, basketball. tennis, ice hockey) by searching by country or league. Both upcoming and past sporting events are available (local matches and top championships).

• Learn statistics: you will see detailed information about the game, the teams, the lineups, the results of past matches, the games progress with key events. Make your predictions. Cheer on your favourite teams! You hold all the cards!

• Subscribe to push notifications. Flexible notification settings allow you to be aware of what is happening. Watch for any games and know about critical moments and results in live mode. Wherever you are - you are in the midst of what is happening!

With the Scores24 app you will not miss a single goal! You will see detailed statistics, schedules, and results of all games online. All in one place - in just one click. Keep in touch with sports 24/7 with Scores24.
