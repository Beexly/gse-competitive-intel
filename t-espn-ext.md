# How do I add my ESPN fantasy league to my account? Why do I need your browser extension to sync my league?

![Dan Martin](https://support.fantasypros.com/system/photos/52811106342939/daniel-martin-jr-headshot.png)



Dan Martin






-
Updated
1 year ago

As of July 2020, due to a change on ESPN's platform, syncing an ESPN fantasy league via our website requires the use of the FantasyPros Chrome Extension. You can still sync your ESPN fantasy league via mobile, but this requires our Draft Wizard or My Playbook app.

**As a reminder, adding a league to your account on one platform (i.e. web or mobile app) makes it available on all platforms.**

[How to sync your ESPN league with the Chrome Extension (web)](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051313453-How-do-I-add-my-ESPN-fantasy-league-to-my-account-Why-do-I-need-your-browser-extension-to-sync-my-league#h_01EE8RVGDJG551ZQBPF5VG0PV6)

[How to sync your ESPN league with the mobile apps](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/360051313453-How-do-I-add-my-ESPN-fantasy-league-to-my-account-Why-do-I-need-your-browser-extension-to-sync-my-league#h_01EE8SKF8FWAQG3G23CT42QQYK)

**Privacy Notice:**

In order for our advice to be based on the latest data for your league, a copy of your encrypted ESPN cookie is securely stored on our servers when syncing via the web. This cookie is used to periodically sync your ESPN league to make sure that rosters and league settings are up-to-date. Please note that this cookie does not contain any human-readable or personally identifiable information.

## How to sync your ESPN league with the Chrome Extension (web)

In order to sync your ESPN league with your FantasyPros account, you must:

1. Be using a laptop or desktop computer.

2. [Install the Google Chrome web browser](https://www.google.com/chrome/) if you do not already have it installed.

3. Allow third-party cookies in Chrome: Go to chrome://settings/cookies in your Chrome address bar, then make sure General settings are set to "Allow all cookies" or "Block third-party cookies in Incognito".

![cookies-chrome.png](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/22073278326683)

4. [Install and activate the FantasyPros Chrome Extension](https://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/extension/). After installation, you should see the FantasyPros browser extension active in Chrome to the right of the address bar.

5. Go to the [league sync page](https://www.fantasypros.com/myleagues/league-sync/site/), select ESPN, and click the **Start League Sync** button. At this point, you’ll be redirected to ESPN.com.

6. On ESPN.com, our Chrome Extension will appear with a list of your leagues that have yet to be synced. Select the leagues you’d like to sync and click **Sync Leagues**. You’ll be redirected back to FantasyPros.com to complete your league sync(s).

7. **If you run into any trouble**, go to [chrome://extensions/?id=gfbepnlhpkbgbkcebjnfhgjckibfdfkc](chrome://extensions/?id=gfbepnlhpkbgbkcebjnfhgjckibfdfkc) in your Chrome address bar and make sure "Allow in Incognito" and "Allow access to file URLs" are both enabled. If these settings are already enabled, you may need to toggle them off and back on.



![](https://support.fantasypros.com/attachments/token/FqN9WNlrYdbw8uFKKbCbiZdPp/?name=inline1125076994.png)




You can quickly check if this resolved the issue by opening this link: [https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/#import\_fp](https://www.espn.com/fantasy/football/#import_fp)

If you see our import panel in the top right​ of the window, the issue has been resolved. Please let us know if you need anything else and we apologize again for the trouble.


Additional step-by-step instructions for syncing with live & mock ESPN drafts [can be found here](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/115001362368-How-do-I-sync-my-ESPN-draft-with-the-Draft-Assistant-).

If you are having issues adding your league via your laptop or desktop computer via Chrome, please try adding the league [via one of our mobile apps](https://www.fantasypros.com/fantasy-mobile-apps/).

## How to sync your ESPN league with the mobile apps

In order to sync your ESPN league with your FantasyPros account, you must:

1. Be using our Draft Wizard or My Playbook mobile app.

2. Tap on the **plus sign** ✚ (My Playbook) or the **Sync** **League** **button** (Draft Wizard).

![](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/37600685313435)

3. Select **ESPN** from the list of league hosts, enter your ESPN credentials (username and password) and tap **Log In**.

4. Select which league(s) you'd like to add and they will populate in the app.
