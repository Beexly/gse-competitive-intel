How an edge looks

Three steps. One honest number.

The AI computes "true" odds from millions of historical matches. Then it compares those to every bookmaker's current price. When a price is too generous, you have an edge.

Model the match

The AI ingests team form, lineups, injuries, rest, venue and historical head-to-head — then outputs a probability distribution across every outcome.

Scan 40+ bookmakers

We pull live odds from every book we follow. Each price implies a different probability.

Surface the edge

When the bookmaker's implied probability is lower than ours, you've got positive expected value. Only those bets appear in the value-bets stream.
