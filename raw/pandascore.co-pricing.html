Are stats plans available for betting-related products?

No, customers with a betting-related usage are not permitted to use the stats plans. Please contact sales@pandascore.co to discuss a tailored plan.

How will you bill me & what form of payments do you accept?

We will bill your credit card monthly, starting on your signup date. We accept all major credit cards - VISA, Mastercard, Diners, Discover, Amex and JCB. Online payments only.

Can I change plans or cancel my account later?

Our monthly subscriptions have a one-month contract. You can cancel, downgrade or upgrade your account at any time.

If you cancel your monthly subscription, it will remain active at its current level through the end of the billing period that you’ve already paid for.
