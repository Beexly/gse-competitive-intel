Frequently Asked Questions

What is a prop betting model?

A prop betting model is an algorithm that projects a player's statistical output - passing yards, points, rebounds, strikeouts - and compares that projection to the sportsbook's line. When the model's projection differs meaningfully from the line, the bet has positive expected value (+EV). Our model runs these projections automatically for every NFL, NBA, and MLB player prop and ranks the board by edge, so you see the most mispriced lines first.

How do your NFL player prop picks work?

Our NFL projection model accounts for matchup quality, pace, weather, injuries, and usage trends to project every player's stat line each week. We compare those projections against live lines from DraftKings, FanDuel, BetMGM, and Caesars and rank every prop by edge percentage. The board updates as lines move, and covers gameday props plus season-long player and team props.

What is a DFS lineup optimizer?

A DFS lineup optimizer uses algorithms to build mathematically optimal daily fantasy sports lineups for DraftKings, FanDuel, and Yahoo. It factors in player projections, salary caps, ownership percentages, stacking correlations, and roster constraints to generate lineups that maximize expected value for both cash games and GPP tournaments.

How does the Fantasy Team Advice DFS optimizer work?

Our optimizer ingests real-time player projections, ownership percentages, Vegas totals, injury reports, and starting lineup confirmations. You set stacking rules, player exposure limits, and contest type - cash or GPP - and the engine generates up to 150 salary-cap-compliant lineups in seconds. Every lineup is built to maximize projected points while balancing ownership leverage.

Can the optimizer build my DFS lineups automatically?

Yes. Set your contest type (cash or GPP), stacking rules, and exposure limits once - the optimizer generates up to 150 salary-cap-compliant DraftKings, FanDuel, or Yahoo lineups in seconds and exports them as a CSV you can upload directly to your contests. Projections, ownership, Vegas totals, and late injury news are already baked in.

What sports does the model cover?

NFL, NBA, MLB, and NHL. During NFL season you get weekly prop projections, gameday and season-long prop pages, and full DFS optimizer support for DraftKings, FanDuel, and Yahoo. NBA season adds our +EV Top Bets board, PrizePicks picks, efficiency ratings, and DvP matchup tools. Every FTA+ membership includes all sports at no extra cost.

Are your player prop tools useful for prediction markets, not just sportsbooks?

Yes. Our projections, edge percentages, and hit-rate tracking are built around the underlying player performance, not a specific sportsbook's odds format - so they work whether you're betting a sportsbook prop or trading a player-performance contract on a prediction market. Compare our projected stat line against whatever line or contract price you're evaluating to find your edge.
