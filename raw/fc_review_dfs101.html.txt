Info and Opinion on FantasyCruncher</h2><p><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class="aligncenter size-full wp-image-3300" src="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/FCLandingPage.jpg" alt="" width="907" height="480" srcset="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/FCLandingPage.jpg 907w, https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/FCLandingPage-300x159.jpg 300w, sizes="(max-width: 907px) 100vw, 907px" /></p><p><strong>What is FantasyCruncher?</strong></p><p>Fantasy Cruncher is a DFS tool that lets you create multiple optimized lineups for the big DFS sites. They call it their <strong>&#8216;Lineup Cruncher&#8217;.</strong></p><p>They work for all the major sports and at the <a href="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/sites/">big DFS sites</a>.<a href="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/go/fantasycruncher/" target="_&quot;blank&quot;" rel="nooper noopener noreferrer"><img decoding="async" class="alignright size-full wp-image-2892" src="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/dfs101-fantasycruncher-logo.png" alt="" width="200" height="120" srcset="https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/dfs101-fantasycruncher-logo.png 200w, https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/dfs101-fantasycruncher-logo-300x180.png 300w, https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/dfs101-fantasycruncher-logo-768x460.png 768w, https://www.dailyfantasysports101.com/wp-content/uploads/2017/10/dfs101-fantasycruncher-logo-1024x614.png 1024w" sizes="(max-width: 200px) 100vw, 200px" /></a></p><p>The biggest thing they have is their &#8216;lineup cruncher&#8217;. It lets you build and optimize mass entry lineups. You can tweak projections, like or dislike players, create any stacking rules you want and FC will make the lineups fit. FC will also put together dozens and dozens optimized lineups based on your input. It&#8217;s user friendly with thumbs up and downs on players.</p><p>It was built organically as a tool a DFS player needed himself <a href="https://www.reddit.com/r/dfsports/comments/2r2t49/fantasy_cruncher_to_become_pay_site/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">and opened up to the Reddit DFS community</a>.</p><p>You use it by tweaking the projections for players. Set the guys you like to higher points projected and lower for the guys you don&#8217;t like. FC does the rest.</p><p>FantasyCruncher puts together optimized lineups for mass entry players. It&#8217;s been called a must have for employing the mass entry strategy to win the big GPP. You want to drop 100 lineups in the quarter arcade?
