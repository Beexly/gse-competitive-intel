      Frequently Asked Questions

      Straight answers on what’s included, how billing works, and how to get help.

      What’s included in a VSiN subscription?

      A VSiN subscription provides subscribers with 24/7 access to our live video stream and full show replays, daily best bets, and full access to VSiN.com that includes pro tools like our exclusive live betting splits and power ratings. Subscribers also receive all of VSiN’s special edition betting guides published during their paid subscription period - annual subscribers receive at least 10 per year, including College Football, NFL, NBA, MLB, World Cup and more.

      What sports does VSiN cover?

      VSiN provides expert betting insight on the NFL, College Football, NBA, College Basketball, MLB, NHL, UFC/Boxing/MMA, PGA golf, tennis, soccer, NASCAR and Horse Racing.

      What’s the difference between the monthly and annual subscription?

      In both subscriptions, you get the same benefits - 24/7 video (live & replays), daily best bets, special edition betting guides, and full VSiN.com access. The annual just saves you 50% off the monthly price by paying upfront for the year.

      What if I start with the monthly subscription and want to switch to the annual subscription to save money?

      Contact us here and we’ll take care of it for you. We’ll credit you if you’ve been charged anything.

      If I buy a seasonal offer, can I switch to another package before my access ends?

      Yes, and you can always contact us here when you’re ready to change plans or renew and we’ll present you with our best offers.

      How do I manage my subscription?

      You can manage your subscription by logging into your My Account page. From there, you can easily update payment information, review transaction history, manage email preferences, and change your password. To cancel your subscription, please contact us here.

      Can I buy VSiN’s Season Guide for my favorite sport separately?

      No, the Season Guides are exclusively for VSiN subscribers. That said, you are welcome to sign up for a shorter or longer subscription period to capture the sport(s) which you find most valuable. There are at least 10 published each year and the best value will always be an annual subscription.

      Can I get access to the 24/7 VSiN channel on TV or on a mobile device without having a full subscription?

      The VSiN TV channel is a core part of a paid VSiN subscription, and most customers choose to access it through their subscription. VSiN does air and stream through numerous national and local broadcasters - for information on how to watch through our partners, go here.

      How much does a VSiN subscription cost?

      You can subscribe to VSiN through monthly, annual, or special sport season-only subscriptions. Check vsin.com/subscribe for our best deals.

      How do I purchase a gift subscription?

      Please contact us here with the contact information for the person(s) to whom you’d like to send a gift subscription.

      Is there a phone number I can call with questions?

      Contact us here and we will call you back.
