Common questions

Can I cancel anytime?
Yes. Cancel from Account → Subscription at any time. You keep your plan's access through the end of your current billing period. No proration, no clawback.
Does my plan cover every sport?
Yes. Every plan (Rookie, Pro, and Sharp) covers every active sport: NFL, NBA, MLB, NHL, CFB, and CBB. There are no per-sport upgrades, ever.
What are AI prop breakdowns and how do tokens work?
Tokens fuel our deep prop analysis. Each full AI breakdown (matchup edge, recent form, line value, recommended action) costs 50 tokens. Rookie includes 2,500 tokens a month (~50 breakdowns), Pro 20,000 (~400), and Sharp 40,000 (~800).
Is there a free trial?
Projections, schedules, injuries and rankings are free to browse with no account at all, so you can judge the data before paying. Plans are month to month and you can cancel anytime.
What happens to my lineups if I change plans?
Your saved lineups stay in your account. Upgrades apply instantly with a prorated charge; downgrades take effect at the end of your billing period, and your new plan's limits apply from there. Re-upgrading restores everything.
Do you offer refunds?
All sales are final. Cancelling stops future billing right away and you keep access through the end of the period you already paid for, but past charges are not refunded, except where required by law. You can cancel anytime from Account → Subscription.
Can I see my past charges?
Yes. Account → Payments shows every transaction with date, amount, plan, and a downloadable receipt.
