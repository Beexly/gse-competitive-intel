    Bruggggggh

    I want everyone to understand that NOTHING is 100% in sports betting. So when rithmm gives you a 90% win probability, it isn’t a true 90% chance. There are certain variables that affect outcomes that rithmm and AI just can’t predict. But when I say this is the closest thing you’ll get to a successful AI prediction for MOST games and MOST outcomes, this app is a GREAT tool for to have that extra edge. It truly helps you find a profitable “Rithmm”. I want to say, don’t just blindly follow the rithmm props. A lot of them will lose. There will be bad days where rithmm props, whether it be MLB, NBA, NFL, whatever, will just got 1-8 (1 win 8 losses). But, on the flip side, there has been days where I’ve seen that number flipped, and there’ll be 8 or even sometimes 9 recommended props that all hit. Not only is this app great for player props, but it’s also well worth the $30 a month for the fact that you can build these things called “models”. These models basically just being built off of what YOU think is the most significant in a game. For instance, let’s take the NBA finals this year. OKC and Pacers. What’s most significant for these finals? Well, pacers have excellent offense, and thunder have excellent defense, so let’s raise those bars up. Who’s on the OKC thunder who foul bates ALL the time? SGA. So let’s slide that foul slider up just a little bit for siakam and SGA. Pace? Well it’s the freaking pacers. Let’s raise that bar up a bit too. Three pointers? Well I’d say that’s just as important as fouls as both of these teams can have poor shooting nights (thunder actually has one of the worst 3 point % this postseason), so let’s lower that. BAM, I just created a model for this whole series. And yes, it really is just that simple. The following picks for each of the games will tailor to this model I created. There’s still a lot more work to do with this app, but I do truly think there’s so much potential that it’s already living up to. Great app, and would recommend trying it for at least a month or two and giving it a chance, and once again, using it as a TOOL to make some money. Thankyou Rithmm!
