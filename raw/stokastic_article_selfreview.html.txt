<h2 style="scroll-margin-top:96px" id="what-the-stokastic-nfl-sims-actually-do">What The Stokastic NFL Sims Actually Do</h2>
<p>Most DFS tools are optimizers. You feed in projections, the optimizer solves for the highest-projected lineup under the salary cap, and you get the median answer to a question that tournaments never ask. A Milly Maker does not pay the median lineup. It pays the lineup that beats a field of hundreds of thousands once.</p>
<p>The Stokastic NFL Sims attack that problem differently. Instead of solving for one lineup, the Sims simulate the contest itself tens of thousands of times: every player&#x27;s outcome varies sim to sim, correlations hold (a QB&#x27;s big game drags his receivers up with him), and projected ownership determines how many entrants you are sharing each player with. Every lineup that comes out is then ranked by simulated ROI against the real payout structure of the contest you tell it you are entering. That last part matters more than people expect. As we walk through in the How To Use Stokastic NFL Sims video embedded above, the &quot;percent to first&quot; setting changes how the Sims value ceiling: a $400,000 contest paying $100,000 to first is 25% to first, and the Sims build differently for that than for a flat double-up.</p>
