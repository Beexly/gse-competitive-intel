The Hidden Costs of Esports Data

If you've ever tried to get pricing for an esports data API, you know the drill: fill out a form, schedule a "discovery call," wait a week, get quoted somewhere between $500 and $5,000 per month.

We surveyed 50+ developers who use esports APIs and compiled their actual costs. The results might surprise you.

The Major Players

PandaScore

  • Starting Price: $500/month (minimum)
  • Enterprise: $2,000-10,000/month
  • Pros: Comprehensive data, 25+ games
  • Cons: Expensive, requires sales call, enterprise-focused

Abios

  • Starting Price: $1,000/month
  • Enterprise: Custom pricing
  • Pros: Good League of Legends data
  • Cons: Very expensive, limited documentation

SportRadar Esports

  • Starting Price: Enterprise only
  • Enterprise: $5,000+/month
  • Pros: Part of larger sports data ecosystem
  • Cons: Not developer-friendly, prohibitive pricing

Cito API

  • Starting Price: Free (500 calls/month)
  • Builder Plan: $50/month (250k calls)
  • Pros: Developer-first, no sales calls, instant access
  • Cons: Focused on 6 supported APIs instead of every esport

What Developers Actually Pay

Based on our survey:

  • Solo developers/hobbyists: Most can't afford any paid API
  • Small Discord bots: $0-50/month (usually free tier or Cito)
  • Startup apps: $200-500/month
  • Established platforms: $2,000-10,000/month

The Real Problem

The esports API market has been dominated by enterprise sales for too long. If you're building a side project or early-stage startup, you're essentially priced out.

This is why we built Cito API. We believe esports data should be accessible to everyone—not just companies with five-figure monthly budgets.

Our Commitment

  • Transparent pricing: Listed on our website, no sales calls
  • Free tier: Always free for hobbyists and learning
  • Fair scaling: Pay for what you use, not what you might use

Conclusion

The esports data industry needs more competition and transparency. We're doing our part by offering enterprise-quality data at indie-developer prices.
