Live during the event, we offer - depending on the sport - each participant's score, the clock, and other headline information. For example, soccer will return the game clock and the half. Baseball will give you the innings, hitter, pitcher, and base situation. For football, down and distance.&nbsp;</p><p><b>Ideal for:</b>&nbsp;score tickers, live dashboards, anything that needs "what's happening right now, and who's winning."</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Live coverage begins the moment a game flips from Scheduled to InProgress, updating roughly 15-20 seconds behind the TV broadcast. Where it matters, we also offer a lighter, Final-only version of this feed alongside the full live-and-final version, so you can trade refresh cost for freshness depending on what your product needs.</p><h3>Team &amp; Player Stats</h3><p>Now we get to the meat of the Event Feeds: the deep, statistical information that fans crave and AI models require. Box scores, season stats, red zone stats, game logs - the full statistical record from dozens of leagues worldwide. Our Data Operations team collects stats as they happen and applies them to a clearly-formatted record for each player in each game.</p><p><b>Ideal for:</b>&nbsp;AI modeling, fantasy scoring and analysis, betting verification, box score pages, statistical leaderboards, and all situational content that requires a keen statistical eye.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Our box scores update at the same cadence as the game, 15-20 seconds behind broadcast. A few select stats may be returned after the game to allow for more detailed review, but the vast majority are updated as they happen, meaning that live box scores are a reality. Season-level stats are aggregated after the games end.</p><h3>Play-by-Play</h3><p>Our most detailed Event Feed, the Play-by-Play feed is the closest thing to experiencing the game live. Detailed event progression for supported competitions - every down on the football field with narrative and statistical data, every pitch on the diamond, every pass, foul and shot in hockey and basketball.</p><p><b>Ideal for:</b>&nbsp;Live game trackers, real-time win-probability graphics, second-screen companion experiences, and after-the-fact breakdowns of how a game actually unfolded.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;From the first play of the game to the last, every play is loaded as it happens, with both a written description and a play-level statistical output for every player involved in that one play. The live variant trails the broadcast by about 15-20 seconds, same as Scores &amp; Game State.</p><h2>Player Feeds</h2><p>Player Feeds give you the information you need on availability and expected participation - the layer that sits on top of rosters and answers "who's actually playing" as gameday approaches.</p><h3>Depth Charts</h3><p>Who's slotted where, in order, for every rostered player expected to play this season. This is agnostic of injury status - a player on IR still shows up here if they're expected back before the season ends.</p><p><b>Ideal for:</b>&nbsp;roster-battle storylines, "who starts if X is out" content, and any tool that needs a running picture of who a team turns to next at a position.</p><p><code>DepthOrder: 1</code>&nbsp;is the current starter at that position; everything below it is who the coaching staff would turn to next.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Updated in real time all year as roster moves, injuries, and positional battles shuffle the order - treat it as the coaching staff's current intent, not a gameday confirmation.</p><h3>Confirmed &amp; Projected Lineups</h3><p>NFL doesn't ship a dedicated "lineup" endpoint the way batting-order sports do. Instead, the starter/inactive question is answered by combining Depth Charts above (who's starting, if healthy) with the Injuries feed below (who's been ruled out for this specific game).</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;DFS lineup-lock tools and batting-order graphics for sports like MLB and NHL that do publish an explicit lineup.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Take NFL as an example. Gameday inactives are released by NFL teams 90 minutes before kickoff; we flip a player to inactive the moment we ingest that report. In sports with an explicit batting or starting order (MLB, NHL), a Projected Lineup is available up to 15 days out, and a Confirmed Lineup lands 2-4 hours before first pitch or puck drop - usually within minutes of the team's official announcement.</p><h3>Injuries</h3><p>Status, body part, practice participation, and notes for every managed injury.</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;sit/start tools, injury-report graphics, and giving betting or fantasy products the context behind a sudden line move or a surprising box score.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Full-roster injury tracking runs during the regular season and postseason; during the offseason and preseason, only players dealing with a serious injury are managed, so a healthy roster shows almost nothing here in July. In-season, teams publish practice-participation reports three times a week - shifting around short-week Thursday-night games - and we update on that same cadence.</p><p><b>A point worth knowing:</b>&nbsp;In many sports, a player's roster status (&nbsp;<i>Active</i>&nbsp;,&nbsp;<i>Injured Reserve</i>&nbsp;,&nbsp;<i>Physically Unable to Perform</i>&nbsp;, …) and their game-day injury designation (&nbsp;<i>Probable</i>&nbsp;,&nbsp;<i>Questionable</i>&nbsp;,&nbsp;<i>Doubtful</i>&nbsp;,&nbsp;<i>Out</i>&nbsp;) are two different systems that don't always agree - a player can carry both at once. This past training camp, Josh Sweat's roster status read "Physically Unable to Perform" while his injury designation separately read "Out" for a knee issue. We keep both fields, joined on the same player, precisely so your product doesn't have to guess which one is telling the truth.</p><h2>Betting Feeds</h2><p>Betting Feeds are built for the businesses closest to the money - sportsbooks, media sites running betting content, and the analytics tools that sit between them. Comparisons, machine learning, and big data metric measurement.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;We have opening, line movement, and closing lines for all the major sports and events; live odds move with the game once it starts.</p><h3>Props</h3><p>We offer props across player, team, game, and miscellaneous categories, aggregated from top sportsbooks across the ecosystem.</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;Odds comparison, AI modeling, expert tips and picks, and anything else that needs the hottest betting data that fans engage with the most.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Populates alongside game lines pre-game; results settle 10-15 minutes after the game, once stats are verified. Settled markets don't just disappear once a game is over - anything older than 30 days rolls into our historical Vault archive, so prop data stays queryable long after the season ends. Note that props are pre-game only; we do not carry in-play props at this time.</p><h3>Futures</h3><p>Outright and season-long markets (championship odds, award odds).</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;championship-odds widgets, award-race trackers, and season-long content that needs to move with team performance rather than a single game.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Live all season, moving with team performance; payouts settle once the outright market resolves - a champion is crowned, an award is handed out.</p><h3>Matchups, Trends &amp; Splits</h3><p>Historical head-to-head data, betting trends, and public betting splits.</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;matchup pages - all the sticky, pre-game context users expect to see once they've found the odds: how these two teams have played before, and which way the public money is leaning.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Splits begin populating shortly after game lines post and the public starts taking action - thinnest early in the week, filling in as kickoff approaches.</p><h2>Fantasy Feeds</h2><p>Fantasy Feeds are built to power your fantasy products - DFS platforms, season-long contests, and the tools built around both.</p><h3>Projections</h3><p>Fantasy and statistical projections, pre-game, per player.</p><p><b>Ideal for:</b> Sticky content for serious fans and DFS players, extra context around pre-game content, betting advice.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Available as soon as the following week's schedule is set, then refreshed continuously through the week as practice reports, injury news, and - closer to kickoff - confirmed inactives change the outlook. Fantasy points and the underlying stat line finalize on the same schedule as Team &amp; Player Stats: provisional live, locked roughly an hour after the game.</p><h2>News &amp; Image Feeds</h2><p>News &amp; Image Feeds give you the editorial content and media assets that keep users on-site and profile pages looking finished.</p><h3>Player News &amp; Notes</h3><p>Short-form player news and updates, timestamped, tied to a player ID for easy joining against roster, projection, and injury data.</p><p><b>Ideal</b>&nbsp;<b>for:</b>&nbsp;profile-page sidebars, push-notification content, and an up-to-the-minute stream of what's happening across a league - headlines, injuries and sit/start context, trades, waiver-wire moves.</p><p><b>Lifecycle:</b>&nbsp;Continuous throughout the day, with volume spiking around practice-report releases, injury news, and gameday.          * Shows JSON in pretty preview
         * @param {object|string} output - what to show

        function encodeHTMLEntities(string) {

                .replace(/</g, '&lt;')
                .replace(/>/g, '&gt;');
        }
