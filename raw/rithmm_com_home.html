07 · Good questions

Frequently asked.

What is the best AI for sports betting?+

The best AI for sports betting is the one that shows its work. Rithmm reads every game and every player prop, compares the number across major sportsbooks, and explains its predictions in plain language, so the decision stays yours. No black box, no hype, just the data and the why. Start with a 7-day free trial and see how the plays hold up.

What is Scout?+

Scout is Rithmm's AI betting buddy, included in Pro and Premium. Ask about any game, player, or bet, and Scout gives you the pick, the edge, and the why — in plain language, backed by Rithmm's predictive models. It lives as its own tab in the app, and as an "Ask Scout" button on any pick or game page.

How do AI sports predictions work?+

AI sports predictions start with data. The models read thousands of data points on every team, player, and matchup, then project a likely outcome and compare it to the market number. Rithmm walks you through that reasoning in plain language, so you understand why a pick holds up instead of just being handed a play.

Are the AI sports picks free?+

You can start free. Every new member gets a 7-day free trial with full access to our free AI sports predictions and tools. After that, the Core plan keeps the picks, player prop analysis, and game analysis coming. It is the simplest way to try the data before you decide.

What sports does Rithmm cover?+

Rithmm covers all the major leagues in one membership: NFL, NBA, MLB, WNBA, and Golf, plus college football and college basketball. You get game analysis, player props, and best parlay bets today across every sport the models track, whether you follow the models our team builds or build your own.

What is Edge?+

Edge shows whether Rithmm's win probability is stronger than the sportsbook's price. It answers one question: Is this bet worth the risk at the current odds? Edge compares two things: Rithmm's win chance (how often the model expects this play to win) and the sportsbook's odds (how much you get paid if the play wins).
