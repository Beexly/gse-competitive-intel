Terminology for betting markets can vary by country, sport and even amongst bookmakers. We aim to simplify this by defining the following markets</p> <table><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left;">Market Key <br>(use in the API)</th> <th style="text-align:left;">Market Names</th> <th style="text-align:left;">Description</th></tr></thead> <tbody><tr><td style="text-align:left;">h2h</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Head to head, Moneyline</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Bet on the winning team or player of a game (includes the draw for soccer)</td></tr> <tr><td style="text-align:left;">spreads</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Points spread, Handicap</td> <td style="text-align:left;">The spreads market as featured by a bookmaker. This market is only applicable to betting exchanges</td></tr></tbody></table> <br> <p><code>spreads</code> and <code>totals</code> markets are mainly available for US sports and bookmakers at this time.</p> <p>For descriptions of these markets, see <a href="/sports-odds-data/betting-markets-examples.html">examples of betting markets</a>.</p> <h2 id="additional-markets"><a href="#additional-markets" class="header-anchor">#</a> Additional Markets</h2> <p>Starting in 2023, more markets are becoming available in the API. Additional markets update at 1 minute intervals.</p> <p>Due to the growing size of the API response, additional markets need to be accessed one event at a time using the new <a href="/liveapi/guides/v4/#endpoint-5"><code>/events/{eventId}/odds</code> endpoint</a>.</p> <table><thead><tr><th style="text-align:left;">Market Key <br>(use in the API)</th> <th style="text-align:left;">Market Name</th> <th style="text-align:left;">Description</th></tr></thead> <tbody><tr><td style="text-align:left;">alternate_spreads</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Alternate Spreads (handicap)</td> <td style="text-align:left;">All available point spread outcomes for each team</td></tr> <tr><td style="text-align:left;">alternate_totals</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Alternate Totals (Over/Under)</td> <td style="text-align:left;">All available over/under outcomes</td></tr> <tr><td style="text-align:left;">btts</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Both Teams to Score</td> <td style="text-align:left;">Odds that both teams will score during the game.
