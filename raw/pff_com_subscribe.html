Frequently Asked Questions

PFF offers three subscriptions. Our monthly subscription costs just $9.99/month and covers all of our premium articles covering fantasy, NFL draft, betting and a lot more NFL and NCAA coverage alongside our fantasy and betting tools. Our PFF+ annual subscription at $119.99 covers all of that plus access to Premium Stats that gives you insights into the same data all of the NFL teams and hundreds of NCAA teams use to prepare for their games. Our tier, designed at experts and power users, is PFF Pro that costs $199.99 and gives you additional stats, data as well as a ready to use API and CLI to work against.

Monthly subscriptions are billed on the same day each month, while annual subscriptions are billed on the same day each year.

Former subscription types, including PFF Edge and PFF Elite, have been folded into PFF+.
