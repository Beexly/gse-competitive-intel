<h1 id="footballcsv---open-public-domain-football-data">football.csv - Open Public Domain Football Data</h1>

<p>What’s the comma-separated values (.csv) format?</p>

<p>It’s the world’s most popular tabular data interchange format in text.
Values are separated - surprise, surprise - by commas (that is, <code class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge">,</code>).
One line is one record. Example:</p>

<div class="language-plaintext highlighter-rouge"><div class="highlight"><pre class="highlight"><code>Team1,                FT,  HT,  Team2
Arsenal,              1-3, 1-1, Aston Villa

Norwich City,         2-2, 0-0, Everton
Sunderland,           0-1, 0-0, Fulham
Swansea City,         1-4, 0-2, Manchester United
West Bromwich Albion, 0-1, 0-0, Southampton
West Ham United,      2-0, 1-0, Cardiff City
