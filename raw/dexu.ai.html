Frequently asked questions

What is Dexu?+

Dexu is a crypto intelligence platform. It continuously indexes Crypto-Twitter/X, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Telegram and Instagram alongside market and on-chain data, then surfaces what moved, who moved it and why — as a dashboard, an AI copilot, or 30 tools you can plug into your own AI.

What is crypto mindshare?+

Mindshare is a project, narrative or account's share of the crypto conversation Dexu indexes over a given window. It's a leading signal for attention rotation — often shifting before price does — and you can track it per project, per narrative, per ecosystem and per KOL.

Which data sources and coverage does Dexu track?+

Six social platforms — X/Twitter, YouTube, TikTok, Reddit, Telegram and Instagram — plus market and on-chain data, re-indexed continuously throughout the day. Coverage spans the top 1,000 tokens by market cap plus tokenless projects with real traction, and every answer states the coverage and freshness behind it.

What is Dexu MCP?+

Dexu MCP exposes 30 read-only tools over the Model Context Protocol, so you can query Dexu's data from Claude, ChatGPT, Cursor and any MCP-compatible AI. It's read-only and never trades, and every answer ships with explicit coverage and freshness.

How is Copilot different from MCP?+

Copilot is the AI chat inside Dexu — it streams grounded answers with inline charts, PDF export and a research mode. MCP takes the same underlying tools out to whatever AI you already use. Same data, two ways in.

Is there a free plan?+

Yes. The free plan is $0 with no card required — 8 MCP tools, 100 credits a month, 4 Copilot messages a day and access to key dashboards. Paid tiers add more tools, more credits, Claude Sonnet and Copilot research mode.
