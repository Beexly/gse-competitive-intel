<p>Every paid Stokastic DFS package is built around <strong>Stokastic Sims</strong>. Instead of ranking players off one static projection, the Sims play out a slate tens of thousands of times and let the results speak. That difference sounds academic until you see what it produces:</p>

Our guide to <a class="MuiTypography-root MuiTypography-inherit MuiLink-root MuiLink-underlineHover css-1d83bhy" href="/articles/dfs-strategy/build-winning-dfs-lineups-stokastic-sims" node="[object Object]">building winning lineups with the Sims</a> goes deep on this.</li>
<li><strong>Ownership As A Distribution, Not A Point Estimate.</strong> Ownership projections show you where the field is crowding and how wide the realistic range runs on each player, so you can decide where being different is worth it.</li>
<li><strong>Top-150 Exposure.</strong> This is the number I check first: how often a player appears in the 150 highest sim-ROI lineups. It&#x27;s an exposure recommendation derived from simulated ROI rather than a gut call, and it regularly disagrees with raw projections in useful ways.</li>
<li><strong>Boom/Bust Probabilities.</strong> Instead of one median number, you see how often a player smashes and how often he craters: the shape of the outcome, not just its middle.
