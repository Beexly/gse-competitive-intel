Yesorno.ai Public Beta Now Live World’s First DeFi+SocialFi+AI-Powered Decentralized Prediction Market Platform

 Category Press Release
April 28, 2026

Revolutionary Binary Options Protocol Transforms Global User Trading on Future Events, Democratizing Prediction Market Access via Blockchain Technology

Netherlands — Yesorno.ai, the world’s first decentralized prediction market platform integrating DeFi, SocialFi, and AI technologies, has officially launched its public beta. The platform enables global users to trade on real-world events — including sports competitions, financial markets, and social trends — through transparent, on-chain binary option contracts. The public beta is now open, inviting users worldwide to experience the next generation of prediction markets.

Transforming Prediction Markets Through Blockchain Innovation

Prediction markets have long served as powerful mechanisms for information aggregation and collective intelligence discovery. As economist Friedrich Hayek proposed in 1945, price mechanisms represent the most efficient form of information aggregation.

Yesorno.ai brings this theory into the digital age, leveraging blockchain to build a transparent, trustless infrastructure for trading future events. The global prediction market is experiencing unprecedented growth. In 2024, decentralized prediction platform Polymarket recorded over $3 billion in trading volume during the U.S. presidential election period, demonstrating the massive potential of blockchain-based prediction markets. Yesorno.ai enters this fast-expanding market with a differentiated approach that addresses key pain points in existing solutions.

Core Platform Features                       

Yesorno.ai stands out with five core innovations designed to lower entry barriers while retaining sophisticated functionality.

Omni-Category Coverage

The platform supports diverse prediction scenarios spanning sports, crypto price movements, financial indices, political elections, and entertainment outcomes. This comprehensive coverage ensures users can access markets aligned with their expertise and interests.

Simplified Binary Options Interface

Yesorno.ai distills complex prediction scenarios into simple Yes/No binary choices. Users do not need expertise in derivatives pricing, margin rules, or complex financial instruments. First-time users can complete their first prediction within 30 seconds, drastically reducing the learning curve vs. traditional platforms.

Full On-Chain Transparency

All transactions, fund flows, and settlement results are recorded on-chain and publicly verifiable. This eliminates trust issues that have long troubled centralized prediction platforms, where fund misappropriation and rule manipulation have cost users billions annually.

AI-Powered Analytics

The platform integrates an intelligent analysis system that aggregates multi-source data, tracks historical trends, and assesses risks in real time. These AI tools act as decision-support assistants, helping users make more informed predictions without replacing human judgment.

SocialFi Integration

Yesorno.ai embeds social features that let users follow top predictors, share insights, and join community discussions. This SocialFi layer turns predictive ability into quantifiable social capital, creating strong network effects that boost platform engagement.

Technical Architecture

Yesorno.ai uses a layered technical architecture that balances security, efficiency, and scalability. Built on Ethereum and other leading Layer 1 blockchains for maximum security and decentralization, the platform also integrates Layer 2 scaling solutions including Polygon to sharply reduce gas fees and improve transaction throughput, ensuring a smooth user experience even during peak volume.

At the protocol level, Yesorno.ai runs on an Automated Market Maker (AMM) engine that removes the need for order book matching. Users trade instantly against liquidity pools, with prices determined algorithmically by pool ratios. The platform charges a 2% transaction fee, of which 1%–1.5% is distributed to liquidity providers as rewards, creating sustainable market-making incentives.

Its proprietary oracle system is a critical infrastructure component. Using multi-source data verification and decentralized consensus mechanisms, the oracle network securely bridges real-world event results to the blockchain for settlement. The standard settlement cycle is 24–48 hours, balancing efficiency and accuracy.

Economic Model and Governance

Yesorno.ai operates a sustainable economic model built for long-term ecosystem growth. Its fee structure ensures platform sustainability while rewarding users who contribute liquidity and value to the network. Platform governance follows a community-driven model: token holders can propose and vote on protocol updates. This decentralized structure ensures development aligns with community interests, not centralized team decisions.

Roadmap and Future Development

Yesorno.ai’s roadmap outlines a clear five-year path from launch to mainstream adoption.

  • Year 1: Focus on platform launch and core feature development; target 10+ active prediction markets and 10,000 users by Q4 2025.
  • Year 3: Support 500 prediction markets across 10+ vertical categories.
  • Year 5: Reach 100 million users globally, establishing Yesorno.ai as the leading decentralized prediction infrastructure.

Risk Disclosure

Yesorno.ai clarifies that prediction markets involve substantial risks, including market volatility, technical vulnerabilities, oracle inaccuracies, and regulatory uncertainty. The platform has implemented comprehensive risk management: smart contract custody for fund security, transparent information disclosure, and formal dispute-resolution mechanisms. Users should only participate with funds they can afford to lose. This announcement does not constitute investment advice.

About Yesorno.ai

Yesorno.ai is a decentralized prediction market protocol combining DeFi, SocialFi, and AI technologies. Built on blockchain infrastructure, it converts real-world events into tradable on-chain binary options, enabling global users to monetize their knowledge and predictive capabilities.

The public beta is currently live. New users can sign up to receive welcome bonuses and participate in prediction markets covering sports, finance, social events, and more.
