# Uruguay vs Cabo Verde Prediction 21 June 2026

Details

The main thing

🔊 Was the African side's performance in the opening round of the [World Cup](https://web.archive.org/web/20260621225514/https://scores24.live/en/soccer/l-international-world-championship/predictions) just a stroke of luck, or a sign of genuine strength? Group H is set to deliver another intriguing clash as Uruguay take on Cape Verde at Hard Rock Stadium in Miami Gardens. Both teams currently have similar odds of advancing from the group, adding extra intrigue to this head-to-head encounter. Still, the gulf in pedigree and star power is clearly reflected in the bookmakers’ odds-Uruguay are given a six-fold advantage over their African opponents.

## UruguayTeam Overview

⚪ South American teams have struggled to find their rhythm at this World Cup, and Uruguay are no exception, dropping points against a clear underdog in their opening match. The two-time world champions - though their glory days are now a distant memory - have had mixed fortunes at recent tournaments, reaching the knockout stage just three times this century. This time around, much of the optimism is pinned on manager Marcelo Bielsa, with the Argentine tactician renowned for getting the most out of limited resources.

🔵 A 1-1 draw with [Saudi Arabia](https://web.archive.org/web/20260621225514/https://scores24.live/en/soccer/t-saudi-arabia-1) felt like a fair outcome, as La Celeste only managed to salvage a point thanks to a late goal from Maximiliano Araujo. The lack of a top-class creative midfielder remains Uruguay’s biggest issue, hampering Bielsa’s tactical approach. The centre-forward position is also a concern - Darwin Nunez was so ineffective in the opener that he was replaced by Agustin Canobbio at halftime.

## Cape VerdeTeam Overview

🔵 The World Cup debutants have already made history after just one matchday. In their clash with [Spain](https://web.archive.org/web/20260621225514/https://scores24.live/en/soccer/t-spain-3) (0-0), the African side committed only a single foul - a new all-time record for the tournament. It’s a remarkable story: since the national federation was founded in 1982, Cape Verde have almost always been considered one of world football’s minnows. Only about 15 years ago did the Blue Sharks begin to make significant strides forward. Now, it looks like Cape Verde have a realistic shot at reaching the knockout stage in their first-ever World Cup appearance.

🔴 Speaking of their debut, it unfolded exactly according to plan. Cape Verde delivered a textbook defensive performance and even had opportunities to snatch a winner late on. Forty-year-old Vozinha, whose nickname translates as “Grandma,” has already etched his name into the history books - his composure and leadership between the posts kept the team steady throughout. Off the pitch, Vozinha has become a fan favorite overnight: his social media following skyrocketed from 20,000 to an astonishing 14.5 million in just one week. Let’s hope this newfound fame doesn’t go to the veteran keeper’s head.

### Cape Verde

🎤 Cabo Verde head coach Bubista didn’t shy away from the praise following his side’s historic draw against Spain: “This means everything for our country. We wanted the world to see what our national team is capable of. We’re resilient. Teams often labeled as underdogs are putting in impressive performances. The level of football has become much more balanced. Being well-organized and structured allows you to compete with the best. Spain may have dominated possession, but that doesn’t mean they controlled the game. Ideally, we’d like to attack more, just as we did towards the end. We don’t lose our composure. Uruguay are a tough opponent, but we always believe in our own style.”

❌ Cabo Verde have no injury concerns - meaning the coaching staff will have their strongest squad available for the upcoming fixture.

🔁 **Update as of June 20:**

- The Cabo Verde coaching staff held a morning video session focused on Uruguay’s recent match. Emphasis was placed on man-marking Federico Valverde, whom Bubista sees as the main threat.
- Cabo Verde’s players note that Miami’s heat and humidity are far more familiar to them than to their South American opponents. The coaching staff believe this climate advantage could help the team withstand Uruguay’s high-intensity pressing.
