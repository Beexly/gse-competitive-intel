# What is Draft Intel?

![Draft_Intel_1200x600.png](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/7844499047707/Draft_Intel_1200x600.png)

[Draft Intel](https://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/football/draft-intel/) is a tool designed to give you insights into how your leaguemates draft. When you [sync](https://draftwizard.fantasypros.com/football/leagues/) a [qualifying league](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/en-us/articles/7846032215323-What-leagues-does-Draft-Intel-support-) with FantasyPros, Draft Intel will analyze up to five years' worth of draft data to discover patterns and tendencies in how your leaguemates prioritize positions, what positions they draft by round, and more.

Some examples of the patterns that Draft Intel will find include:

- _96% of their picks in the first_ **_5 rounds_** _were_ **_WRs_** _and_ **_RBs_** _._
- _Has never drafted a_ **_TE_** _before_ **_Rd 6_** _._
- _Has drafted_ **_WR_** _in_ **_Rd 3_** _in 4 out of 5 drafts._
- _Hasn’t drafted a backup TE in 60% of drafts._

_![image__18_.png](https://support.fantasypros.com/hc/article_attachments/7845257918875/image__18_.png)_

## **Draft Intel in the Mock Draft Simulator**

The patterns discovered in Draft Intel can also be used in the Mock Draft Simulator. On the Draft Intel page, toggle any (or all) patterns you would like the team bots to use in a mock draft. Customize each team to test out drafting _with_ different strategies, and drafting _against_ different strategies.

The Mock Draft Simulator has always been the most accurate mock drafting tool available. Now, with Draft Intel, your mock drafts are _tailored directly to your league_. After deciding which patterns you want to use, Draft Intel will automatically apply those behaviors to the Mock Draft Simulator. From there, you can begin experiencing mock drafts that feel like your real draft!
