Google has updated AI Mode for fantasy sports including adding in integration with FantasyPros. And OpenAI has improved the GPT-5 Instant model for signed-out users.

Nick Fox from Google wrote on [X](https://x.com/thefox/status/1981178680291578316), "Just shipped some improvements to AI Mode for fantasy football season, including an integration with FantasyPros."

"If you're trying to figure out who to start/sit, AI Mode can bring in real-time updates and stats to help you out. Hopefully this advice for my team ages well," he added.

He then linked to \[ [should I start darius slayton, mark andrews, juwan johnson, david njoku or jerome ford in my flex spot for my 2 qb ppr league?](https://www.google.com/search?smstk=ChhlMFVrTG1JQnFPd2lBMDVjU3YwakFBQT0QAQ%3D%3D&smstidx=0&q=should+I+start+darius+slayton%2C+mark+andrews%2C+juwan+johnson%2C+david+njoku+or+jerome+ford+in+my+flex+spot+for+my+2+qb+ppr+league%3F&udm=50&csuir=1&aep=34&shndl=37&shmd=H4sIAAAAAAAA_3WPsU4DMRBEgTKfQLU1is4ECYo01PkKay92bF_sXbNr55KGH-GP-CruaBHd6I1GM7P5eth8auSeHRxAG0oDh5K6gma8NaYtFJQzIDnxs25h6jMSTBxJV9PhJTmgic8dWGDywsXDicVBIig3OGV_Ba3cVriCF_gYoVaB7DF0__74GlurujdmnuchLBtaOg5HLkYjijeYiv1VtsVeRsKUh0rh6e773v6XTAWDVzPKMjtRMIE5ZB9sEHTJUzO76x9mlzwt353dvT27unb8ALgsiPseAQAA&shmds=v1_AdeF8KhLdOrciqZa7wwJpO2ImY-XynDc2_RjOjH96wbwp9Yu1g&source=sh%2Fx%2Faim%2Fm1%2F1&kgs=d8d1ecfd396d1e70&mtid=Kvz5aNnDJ43v5NoPgL2Y4Qw)\] query on AI Mode.

![Google Ai Mode Sports](https://images.seroundtable.com/google-ai-mode-sports-1761213714.png)

OpenAI [wrote](https://help.openai.com/en/articles/6825453-chatgpt-release-notes#h_62abac82cc), "We’re updating the model for signed-out users to GPT-5 Instant, giving more people access to higher-quality responses by default."

So those are two smaller updates for AI Search across Google and OpenAI.
