## Signature tool methodology (from public explainers)

### SMASH — "The Ultimate Fantasy Tool"
- Public definition: *"a proprietary combination of batted ball data, pitch mix, zone data etc., that shows how a player is performing compared to the league average."*
- Philosophy: *"SMASH focuses on the skills of the player directly, versus the results."* (skills over results)
- Color scale: **Green = target, White = average, Red = avoid** (lighter shades for marginal).
- Windows: **(S) = Season**, **(30) = last 30 days**.
- **Advantage Score**: hitters compared vs the ENTIRE opposing pitching staff (SP + relievers); pitchers vs the full opposing lineup.
- Separate proprietary formulas for hitters and pitchers. MLB SMASH page tagline: color legend "RED - Bad for Hitters. GREEN - Good for Hitters."
- NFL SMASH tagline: *"From the mind of Jeff Mans. NFL smash ratings for every team."* Variants: Ratings, Matchups, WR Coverage.
- Detailed calc gated behind Elite Data.

### BURR Report — Bullpen Usage & Reliever Ratings
- Aggregates **14 bullpen categories** (workload/usage, save success, strike/walk rates, batted-ball data) into **one number vs league average**.
- Scale: **<1.00 (Green) = below-average pen = good for batters**; 1.00 = average; **>1.00 (Red) = tough for batters**.
- Rationale: relievers throw more innings than ever in the modern game.

### Ray Flowers "How to Analyze a Pitcher/Hitter" (9th annual)
- Public framing only (framework paywalled): "skills > results," ~5,000-word "blueprint to get to the core of who a player is." Pitcher piece flags **workload** (IP jump, pitch counts, age, late-season shutdowns).
