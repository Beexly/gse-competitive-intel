Positioning — a personality-and-community moat

FantasyGuru sells expertise and belonging first, data second. Two on-air personalities anchor the brand and a 24/7 Discord keeps members inside it.

  • Brand claim: "Expert Analysis, Strategy, and Fantasy Tools to more than 100k members for more than 20 years." Entity: Guru Fantasy Reports, Inc., © 1995–2026.
  • Distribution moat: SiriusXM Fantasy Sports Radio — "Elite Sports with Jeff Mans," Channel 87, Mon–Fri 2–5 PM ET. Part of the Elite Sports Network / EliteFantasy group. GSE has no equivalent broadcast pipeline.
  • Two faces: Jeff Mans (NFL, Owner/Chief Content Officer) and Ray Flowers (MLB, EVP EliteFantasy, full-time since 2001). Rob Brink is Owner/CEO. GSE's own "beat Jeff Mans" benchmark points straight here.
  • Community: members-only 24/7 Discord + "Daily Office Hours with experts," pushed on every tier — a relationship moat, not a data one.
  • Breadth: NFL · MLB · NBA · NHL · NCAAB · PGA · MMA · Soccer · NASCAR · Horse Racing · CFB · CFL · UFL. NFL & MLB are the two flagship paid tracks.

Signature methodology (from public explainers — what they reveal to sell it)

Flagship metric

SMASH — "the WAR of fantasy baseball"

"A proprietary combination of batted-ball data, pitch mix, zone data… vs league average." Core creed: "focuses on the skills of the player directly, versus the results."

Green = target · White = average · Red = avoid. Windows: (S) season, (30) last-30. "Advantage Score" grades a hitter vs the entire opposing staff. Exact calc gated.

Reliever index

BURR — Bullpen Usage & Reliever Ratings

Since 2017. Rolls 14 bullpen categories into one number vs league average: <1.00 (green) = weak pen, good for hitters; >1.00 (red) = tough.

Proprietary stat

"Solds" = Saves + Holds

Ray Flowers' reliever-value metric, pitched publicly as a replacement for "the standard outdated saves category." Drives the Closer Grid + Solds Rankings.

NFL model

QB Types (mobility)

Mike Horn classifies starters as "Very Mobile / Running" on the thesis that running QBs score more — a stated +2–4 fantasy points/game edge. Feeds draft cheat-sheets.

Live opening: the MLB Umpire Report notes FantasyGuru dropped Swish Analytics in 2026 ("no longer provides the stats I once used") and rebuilt around home-plate ump tendencies + K-props + team K-rates by handedness. A competitor with durable strike-zone/pitch sourcing can contest that product now.

Where FantasyGuru is strong, respect it; where it's exposed, GSE's trust-first, prove-it thesis has clean wedges.

Opportunity

Open the black box

SMASH/BURR are sold as opaque "proprietary" numbers. GSE's glass-box, cited-source, calibrated approach is the direct counter — show the reasoning they hide.

Opportunity

Publish an audited hit-rate

FG posts picks but never a public, audited win-rate. GSE's ≥70% "prove-it-don't-fake-it" north star attacks exactly that trust vacuum.

Opportunity

Their vendor just churned

They lost Swish Analytics for umpire/strike-zone data. Durable strike-zone/pitch sourcing makes the umpire-edge product contestable right now.

Opportunity

Undercut the "pay-again" data tax

Elite Data is $19.99/mo on top of a $199–499 plan. Bundling transparent, calibrated data undercuts the model — and the confusing dual pricing.

Threat

Personality + radio distribution

Jeff Mans / Ray Flowers on SiriusXM Ch.87 + a 24/7 Discord is a relationship moat GSE can't buy quickly. Compete on trust & proof, not fame.

Threat

46k+ articles, near-daily coverage, and sticky named tools (SMASH/BURR/Solds) members already trust. Branding the number matters — mirror it with GSE Rating.

Formats worth emulating (as templates, not copies): Coach Breakdowns (system→player impact), O-line Breakdown, Umpire/Strike-Zone report, SMASH-style matchup heatmap, BURR bullpen index, "Solds" reliever metric, QB-types mobility model, Targets & Touches usage tracker, Closer Grid, FAAB Values, MLB Planner.

Below are live 2026 outputs from gse_engine.py. 311 relievers, 30 bullpens, 463 hitters, 337 pitchers scored.

◇ Why this is clean & better

Baseball stats are uncopyrightable facts; "Saves+Holds," league-normalized indices, and expected-stats matchup models are methods, which copyright doesn't protect. Every input is public and free. Unlike FG's sealed numbers, every component and weight here is visible and back-testable — the glass-box counter to their black box, and IP you own.
