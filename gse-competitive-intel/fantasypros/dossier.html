01

Positioning — an aggregator, not a modeler

FantasyPros' entire product is other people's opinions, averaged and distributed at scale. Its signature — ECR (Expert Consensus Rankings) — is a blend of ~90–100+ experts from 100+ outlets. It has no first-party predictive signal of its own.

  • The core: ECR = the mean of the field; the "Expert Accuracy" leaderboard is a grading layer that legitimizes it; everything else (projections, Draft Wizard, My Playbook, DFS) is tooling wrapped around the consensus + league-sync.
  • The crux for GSE: you can't out-aggregate them (they own the field) or cheaply out-SEO them. You beat them on the one thing consensus cannot produce — a differentiated, calibrated, provable prediction. Their own accuracy engine literally can't detect when the whole field is wrong (§07, seam 6) — a real model can.
The smoking gun — aggregation all the way down: even their own projections are averages of others. Zeile, their consensus projection, is by their own description a straight-up average of the other projection systems — and it won their own 2025 baseball projection-accuracy study (the crowd mean beat every individual modeler). ATC is an accuracy-weighted average; the real models they blend (THE BAT, etc.) are third parties'. ECR averages rankings (Borda-count "Rank Points"); Zeile averages projections. FantasyPros manufactures zero first-party prediction. Bonus: ATC beating Zeile — accuracy-weighting the sources beats a plain average — validates GSE's accuracy-weighted-consensus engine with their own data.

Corporate reality — bootstrapped, lean, marketing-born

A ~4M-page, 38M-visit business run by fewer than 20 people with zero outside capital — only possible because the content is templated and the intelligence is borrowed.

  • Founded 2010 by David Kim (CEO) & Tom Nguyen — both from Hotwire (online marketing / marketing analytics). Founder DNA is performance marketing, not sports science — which is exactly why it's an SEO machine.
  • Entity: Marzen Media LLC, Las Vegas NV. Also owns BettingPros.com.
  • Bootstrapped — no VC/PE on record. Grown via tuck-ins: Pickemfirst LLC (2013), Sports Injury Alert (2015).
  • Scale (3rd-party est.): ~$5–10M revenue, 10–19 employees; own claim 38M annual visitors.
Read: they won't out-R&D a focused, funded modeling effort. Their advantage is inertia + SEO breadth + league-sync lock-in — not data-science velocity. That's a moat you route around, not out-spend.

06

The two signature systems

Consensus

ECR — Expert Consensus Rankings

Not a simple average — a "Rank Points" system per player by ranked position, to avoid unranked-player skew. Exposes Best/Worst/AVG/StdDev + ADP + "vs. ADP." A "Pick Experts" control recomputes from a chosen subset. Types: Draft/Weekly/ROS/Dynasty/Rookies/Waiver/Sleepers. Formats: Std/Half/PPR/Superflex/2QB/BestBall.

Credibility engine

Expert Accuracy (verified)

Rank slot → expected pts (historical avg) → |expected−actual| = Accuracy Gap. Pool = union of top-N by ECR & top-N by actual. Weekly gaps → z-scores vs field → drop each expert's worst-z week (after Wk8) → sum Wks 1–17. Half-PPR. 150+ experts in-season, 190–225+ preseason. MLB = 5×5 roto.

Verified against the live page and the 2018 Wayback archive: the Accuracy-Gap + z-score + drop-worst-week skeleton has been stable since ≥2018 — they only widened pools (RB 35→40, WR 35→50), switched Standard→Half-PPR, and split out IDP. The entire "most accurate experts" brand has rested on this the whole time.
07

The accuracy loopholes — the corner worth prying open

Their credibility rests on a grader with six exploitable seams. The z-score design is genuinely clever — and genuinely biased toward chalk.

1
Rank→generic-curve baseline ignores conviction. Two experts who rank a player the same score identically — tiering and gaps (the real signal) are invisible. Rewards ordinal guessing, not calibrated magnitude.
2
Drop-worst-z-week launders the biggest blowup while z-scoring caps upside → structurally rewards low-variance chalk-huggers, which then biases the whole ECR toward chalk.
3
Omission seam (verified, narrower than first thought). They defend it, but the penalty for omitting a boomer is capped at ~ECR+1, not his actual finish — so omitting deep sleepers still costs far less than the miss is worth.
4
Snapshot timing = free accuracy. Two fixed snapshots reward whoever updates latest (info timing), not foresight.
5
Chalk-week z-noise. Tiny raw-gap spreads get magnified into big z-swings — leaderboard motion that is statistical noise presented as skill.
6
Relative, not absolute (the deepest). A z-score says "better than the field," never "actually right." Consensus-wide error is undetectable and unpenalized — being wrong with everyone is free. Exactly the error a differentiated model exists to catch.

Full detail: fp-accuracy-loopholes.md + fp-zscore-deepdive.md (with the 2018→2025 diff).

09

Strategic read for Galaxy Sports Edge

Opportunity

Beat consensus where it's blind

ECR is a mean; its accuracy engine can't see consensus-wide error (seam 6). A calibrated, absolutely-scored model catches exactly that — and it's provable.

Opportunity

Publish an honest accuracy board

Their z-score/drop-worst-week method rewards chalk and launders blowups. A proper scoring rule (Brier/log-loss, calibration, no dropped week, coverage-adjusted) is a fairer scoreboard — and a public one undercuts their core credibility asset.

Opportunity

Own the number

They sell the field's opinion; GSE sells its own rating with receipts. "We don't average pundits — we out-predict them, here's the back-test" is a pitch consensus can't answer.

Respect

Distribution + lock-in

Massive SEO breadth, ESPN/Yahoo/Sleeper/CBS league-sync lock-in, a huge free funnel — run absurdly lean. Compete on signal & trust, not page count.

Formats worth emulating (templates, not copies): ECR-style dispersion display (Best/Worst/StdDev), "vs. market" divergence (ADP/line), Strength-of-Schedule grades (GSE has the scheme/defense engine), Closer Depth Chart (GSE has Solds/BURR), Draft-Wizard-style simulation, and an accuracy leaderboard done honestly.
