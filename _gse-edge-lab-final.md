## ★ HONEST CEILING
BLIND, all-games rate is capped ~52-56% ATS-equivalent and will NOT beat an efficient closing line — the no-vig Pinnacle close is too sharp and the market-conditional information I(features;Y|Q_close) in free public data is small. The only legitimate '~60%' is a SELECTIVE number: realistically 57-60% realized on roughly 8-15% coverage (props-heavy plus soft/early game lines), and it may be CLAIMED only after a true multi-season purged walk-forward shows positive CLV over 200+ fired bets — never on ~50, which is enough to detect CLV but far too few to distinguish a hit-rate from the 52.4% breakeven. The corresponding CLV is the real deliverable: +100 to +300 bps vs OBTAINABLE price / soft books (DK/FD) on the fired subset, but only break-even-to-slightly-positive vs the PINNACLE close. Sell CLV-vs-obtainable-price as the headline; CLV-vs-Pinnacle is the internal proof bar, not a marketing number. Any number stated without its coverage denominator, a Wilson/Clopper-Pearson lower bound, and CLV backing is fabrication by omission.

## The selective-~60 path
The path is SORT-AND-GATE, not a better full-slate guess. (1) Produce a genuinely calibrated probability p(x) (beta/temperature tail on out-of-fold predictions). (2) Compute the calibrated EDGE over the no-vig line e=p-q — this is the firing signal, NOT distance-from-boundary confidence kappa (a 0.90 favorite has kappa=0.90 and ZERO edge because the line already prices it; selecting on kappa fires on chalk at negative EV). (3) Fire only when a conformal lower bound LCB(e)>tau_vig, tau tuned on a DISJOINT walk-forward fold, reported with a Wilson LCB. As tau rises, coverage falls and selective accuracy rises monotonically — that risk-coverage curve IS the 60% engine, bounded above by how soft the specific lines are, not by the statistics. HOW BIG is the ~58-62% slate: on GAME lines alone it is tiny — ~8-15% of ~272 NFL games is only ~20-40 fired plays/season, below the single-season significance floor. The subset that actually runs at scale is PROPS-HEAVY: thousands of weekly prop lines, ~25-40% shrinkage-eligible, is where partial-pooling on soft prop pricing supplies enough real, fireable edge to populate a 57-60% subset with meaningful volume, supplemented by soft/early game lines from closing-line distillation. Proof is CLV (converges ~50-200 bets), not hit-rate (needs ~1000+).

## Overfitting guards
- Purged + embargoed walk-forward CV (Lopez de Prado) on every model — overlapping game windows and player autocorrelation leak across naive k-folds; one most-recent season held as an untouched forward holdout until sign-off.
- As-of feature store with a hard cutoff at the decision timestamp; a mandatory shuffled-time placebo that must drive CLV to 0. The closing line must NEVER enter features and 'entry' odds must be genuinely available at decision time, never backfilled.
- Multiple-testing control: Deflated Sharpe / White Reality Check / Hansen SPA on base-model admission, Benjamini-Hochberg FDR on feature search, Learn-then-Test FWER-bounded threshold selection, and an honest registry of EVERY threshold/feature tried (an incomplete registry voids the guarantee).
- Threshold tau and operating points tuned ONLY on inner/disjoint folds and confirmed on the untouched holdout — tuning the risk-coverage curve on the eval set is guaranteed optimism, the single biggest self-deception risk.
- Select on calibrated edge e=p-q, not confidence kappa (kappa fires on correctly-priced chalk at zero EV); all EV math post-vig against the no-vig implied probability.
- Ledoit-Wolf covariance shrinkage + James-Stein edge haircut + fractional Kelly (lambda 0.25-0.35) so Sigma^-1 estimation error cannot produce insane leverage; CLV deflator ties stake to realized rho_CLV with a prior rho_CLV->0 until ~50-100 settled bets.
- Beta/temperature parametric tail instead of pure isotonic in the sparse high-confidence region; Brier reliability-resolution decomposition, not fixed-bin ECE (optimistically biased), as the calibration acceptance metric.
- Adaptive Conformal Inference + Wasserstein covariate-shift monitor for non-stationarity — re-fit rolling, embargo across rule regimes (MLB shift ban, NFL coverage rules), retire any signal/cell whose realized lead-time or IC collapses instead of rationalizing it.
- Hierarchical/partial-pooling priors everywhere small-sample (per-market weights, mesh channel precisions, umpire EB rates) — never per-cell point estimates; freeze weights on a true holdout rather than re-chasing per season.
- Prove with CLV (converges ~50-200 bets), never short-run settled hit-rate (~1000+ bets to separate from breakeven); every published number carries coverage + LCB + walk-forward provenance.

## What would make this fail
- Look-ahead leakage: any closing-line-derived or post-decision feature entering x_t makes closing-line distillation and the latency models circular — a fake edge that evaporates live. The most likely silent fatal bug in the program.
- No genuine market-orthogonal information: if I(features;Y|Q_close)~=0, the raw model does not diverge from the close, and calibration/conformal/selection just abstain their way to zero coverage. The ceiling is market inefficiency, not the math — no statistical trick manufactures edge that isn't there.
- Selling the selective ~60% as a blind/full-slate rate, or claiming it before a multi-season holdout on 200+ fired bets — this re-enters the fabricated-70% territory the founder rejected and destroys the trust-first brand.
- Non-stationarity / reflexivity: books close faster and sharper every season (edges decay 20-40%/yr), and as GSE's CLV edge becomes real it partly gets absorbed into the very close it trains on — the edge is self-limiting and must be continuously re-earned.
- Multiple-testing false discovery: dozens of levers x market types x threshold grids x feature sets is a false-discovery machine; without FWER/FDR control and a complete registry, the luckiest threshold gets sold and reverts out-of-sample.
- Small-sample illusion: a 24-4 hot streak or a thin mesh cell read as a 60% edge; props/latency spots settle slowly so CLV feedback is months-long and small-sample — promoting a market type on <50 OOS bets is fabrication.
- Capacity and execution reality: the softest markets where edge is largest are exactly where books limit/void sharp action; the latency edge is machine-speed-bound and GSE places no real money, so paper CLV on stale alt-lines and news windows overstates anything harvestable.
- Chasing fashionable architecture (GNNs, exotic base learners, do-calculus causal claims) that ~17 NFL games/season cannot support — spending the build budget on novelty-that-isn't-edge instead of the calibration+selection+sizing wrapper and the props specialist where the real realized-edge-per-hour lives.
- Compliance failure: live SiriusXM Ch87/Airwave capture without legal sign-off, or scraping proprietary feeds — the signal-mesh work must stay passive-public/licensed and founder-gated or the program is legally dead regardless of edge.
