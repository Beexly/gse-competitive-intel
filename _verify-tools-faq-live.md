**[Fantasy Strength of Schedule](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/strength-of-schedule.php)** Which fantasy players have the easiest or most difficult fantasy schedules for the coming season? We have the answers in our team-by-team Strength of Schedule tool, which displays the relative ease or difficulty of each NFL team’s matchups for the season. SOS is based on each opponent’s fantasy points allowed to a position, adjusted for strength of schedule. Favorable matchups receive better matchup star ratings. You can learn more about the methodology [here](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl-matchup-ratings/).

**[Fantasy Depth Charts](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/depth-charts.php)** You need to know each teams’ pecking order come draft day – so let us help you with our easy-to-follow Depth Charts tool, which is updated frequently – and includes each player’s overall Expert Consensus Rankings, so you know which backups are worth watching the most as you get into the latter stages of your fantasy draft.

**[Value-Based Drafting Tool](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/rankings/vbd.php)** Not down with VBD? Well, you should be! We offer a detailed look at each player’s standing using value-based drafting (VBD), a strategy that assigns a value to every player by comparing that player’s fantasy points to the fantasy points of a baseline player at the same position. A high VBD means a player offers strong incremental value at his position and is therefore valuable overall in fantasy. Check out our [Value-Based Drafting Guide](https://www.fantasypros.com/2017/06/what-is-value-based-drafting/) for more VBD insight.

**[Fantasy Matchup Calendar](https://www.fantasypros.com/nfl/matchups/qb.php)** Get a handle on just what kind of schedule your fantasy players have on the horizon with out Fantasy Matchup Calendar, which assigns each player a grade out of five stars based on the difficulty of their upcoming games. Ratings are based on a combination of opponents’ points against (adjusted for SOS) and the player’s overall skill level.
