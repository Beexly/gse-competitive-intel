# Are salary cap simulations supported?

![AJ Adams](https://support.fantasypros.com/system/photos/7342684956955/Headshot.jpg)



AJ Adams






-
Updated
3 years ago

Yes, salary cap simulations are supported for NFL and MLB leagues. In order to take advantage of this feature along with other enhanced Draft Wizard® functionality, you will need to upgrade to our [MVP, HOF, or GOAT membership](https://secure.fantasypros.com/plans/).

Additionally, our Draft Assistant, Cheat Sheet Creator, and Draft Analyzer are all compatible with salary cap drafts for NFL and MLB leagues.

Salary cap simulations are not supported for NBA leagues at this time.
